## Outline

### Introduction
Most scheduling platforms do a great job managing appointments, but the intake experience is often treated as an afterthought. For practices that care about professionalism and client experience, that's a missed opportunity.

### Why Providers Outgrow Built-In Forms
Long, cluttered forms. Generic appearance. Poor mobile experience. Multiple signatures. Difficult editing. Limited branding.

### Why Client Experience Matters
First impressions. Completion rates. Building trust. Reducing day-of paperwork. Creating a premium experience.

### What to Look for Instead
Mobile-first design. One-question-at-a-time flow. Branding. Conditional questions. Digital signatures. Easy provider management.

### A Better Approach
Introduce DBC's philosophy here, experience-first, not feature-first: "Modern digital intake software should feel like part of your brand, not an administrative hurdle. The best systems help clients complete forms quickly while giving providers the information they need before the appointment begins."
